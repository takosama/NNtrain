- Deterministic synthetic fixed-seed tokens and fresh model; no dataset/tokenizer/checkpoint/metrics I/O. Each full-step number is a synchronous wall-clock p50; final comparison uses 2 warmup + 4 measured optimizer updates. Device-kernel timings are profiling events and must not be added to overlapping host phases.

- Direct replay's fresh/saved probability, statistics, and derivative comparison passed across causal mask modes 0/1/2, with masked signaling NaNs and multiple head offsets.
- End-to-end T2048 forward and two accumulated backward passes passed bitwise comparisons with direct replay off/on for float32 and mix8_32, dense and causal (4 cases), with no implicit D2H inside the operation.
- Other candidate focused tests cover head/batch boundaries, causal tails, accumulation, and bitwise output; failed performance candidates remain disabled.
- Baseline, candidate, and final full-step artifacts: `benchmark-results/arc-config-t2048-b16-a8-20260922.json`, `benchmark-results/arc-attn-base-t2048-20260923.json`, and `benchmark-results/arc-attn-final-t2048-20260923.json`.

With those experimental cases included, four small-shape FlashAttention acceptance cases still fail independently of the T2048-only dispatch; an unrelated pre-existing Float16 operation inventory omission was found and corrected in this task, then passed its focused test. A real FineWeb epoch was intentionally not run; the measured figure excludes input pipeline and checkpoint I/O.

## Remaining high-impact avenue (not implemented)

The current separate backward dP, derivative, dQ and dK/dV stages remain the major opportunity. A row-delta identity could compute `delta = dY · O_raw` and form `dS = P * (dP - delta) / sqrt(D)` inside the dP epilogue, eliminating the expensive standalone derivative pass. It requires an unquantized FP32 forward output: retaining all layers would add roughly 2 GiB to this already 9.15 GiB peak, while recomputing it in backward costs about 2.75 GPU seconds/update before any saving. More importantly, `dY · (P V)` and `sum(P * (dY V^T))` associate FP32 additions differently. No accuracy or full-step result was obtained for this design here, so it is not enabled and is not counted as an optimization.
